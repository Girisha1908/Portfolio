The framework that I usually follow for designing an outbound gtm strategy for a B2B product starts with Research.

1. company and market analysis
	1. identify the core products and services
	2. revenue model
	3. geographical presence
	4. technology stack

2. market landscape
	1. Calculate the TAM, SAM & SOM
	2. identify the tapped segments and untapped oppurtunities
	3. buyer journey and decision making
	4. seasonality and cycles/ regulatory environents

3. value proposition analysis
	1. strongest value story? with supported fact
	2. identify pain points of each segment and value offered by company
	3. funnel company optimizes
	4. competitive advantage
	   
4. competitive intelligence
	1. direct competitors( vendor/pricing model/positioning/strengths vs yours)
	2. indirect competitors/ alternatives (why buyers choose it?/ threat level)
