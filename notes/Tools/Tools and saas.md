### 2. to map tam- discolike- https://discolike.com/

**[Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/) is fundamentally built on a "people graph"data sourced heavily from LinkedIn, while** **[DiscoLike](https://discolike.com/) is a search engine built on an independent crawl of google.

how gtm engineers use them

1. They use **DiscoLike** to find a highly specific, clean list of target company domains that match an exact niche or website criteria.

2. They export that domain list via CSV or [Clay](https://www.clay.com/).

3. They drop those domains into **Apollo** to map out the individual decision-makers (e.g., CEOs, VPs) and drop them into an email sequence.
